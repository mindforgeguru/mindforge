How an online test behaves
PropertyBehaviour
AttemptsOne attempt per student.
WindowOpen for three days from the moment the teacher publishes it.
DeadlineSubmits on its own when the window closes, so nothing is lost.
GradingAutomatic on submission. The teacher reads and marks the written answers herself.
ResultGoes straight into marks, where the parent sees it the same day.
