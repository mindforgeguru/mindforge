Mind Forge Landing page — three options to choose from

Three directions for
the MindForge landing page.

All three carry the same content: a sign-in front door for admin, teacher and student, then a short explainer. All three are light mode only, work down to 360px, and have their own moving graphics. Open each, then tell me which one to take to finish.

Every sign-in and app button is a placeholder until the real URLs are supplied. Screens, marks and papers are illustrative samples, not real student records.

Option A

Whiteprint

The school day drawn as an engineering whiteprint. Three role doors are three ports drawn in section through the forge, with a heat band running the five stages of a test.

Moving graphics
Drifting construction grid, a dimension line that extends itself, the heat band breathing, embers rising off the anvil.
Type
Rajdhani · Karla · Spline Sans Mono
Feels like
Precise, technical, confident. Closest to your logo's navy-and-orange.

Answer Booklet

The page is the exam answer booklet and its OMR sheet — the object every student, teacher and school office already handles by hand. The three roles are three boxes you fill to enter.

Moving graphics
A cyan scan band reading down the sheet, OMR bubbles inking in sequence, bubbles filling as you pick a role.
Type
Zilla Slab · Lexend · Courier Prime
Feels like
Warm, familiar, unmistakably an Indian school. The safest of the three.

Step Row

The school day as a step row on a light instrument panel — eight period keys under a running chase light, three role banks beside them, and a dot-matrix readout naming what it holds.

Moving graphics
The most of the three. A chase light sweeping the period row, a dot-matrix readout stepping, LEDs lighting under the pointer — all on one clock.
