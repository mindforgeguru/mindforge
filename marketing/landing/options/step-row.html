What the
record holds.

Each line below is a thing the school already records on paper, kept in the same place instead. One login covers all of them.

01TestsSet from a chapter by the teacher, attempted on a phone, graded on submission. One attempt, three-day window, auto-submit at the end.
02HomeworkWhat was given, in which subject, by when, and whether it has been done.
03MarksEvery result as it is graded, and the report that adds them up at term's end.
04AttendanceMarked daily by the class teacher, counted for the month, visible at once.
05TimetablePeriod by period, including the day it changes, for the class and the teacher both.
06FeesWhat's due, what's paid, and a receipt that can be downloaded and kept.
07NoticesSent once by the school, delivered to every parent's phone as a notification.
