03

Decide how you'll handle children's data

Blocker

This is a legal call, not a coding one. Your app holds real records about minors. Different regions have rules about that — the US has COPPA, Europe has GDPR-K, India has the DPDP Act — and they're mostly about getting proper consent from parents. No code can answer this; it's a decision you make, ideally with someone who knows the law where your first schools are.

What you do

  1. Write down which countries or regions your first schools are in.
  2. Ask a lawyer (or a legal-advice service) what those regions require for children's data and parental consent.
  3. Keep a one-page written answer for each region — it's what a store reviewer or a school may ask to see.
✓ Done when: you have a written position for every region you'll operate in.
You + a lawyer a few days (mostly waiting)
